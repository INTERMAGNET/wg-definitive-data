### regarding 3.c) messages (automatic and non-automatic) - privacy notice

Request from BGS: BGS would like to add a personal data privacy notice to all communications leading to data being sent to the INTERMAGNET repository

Suggestion: add the following message to the IMBOT publication message, add the same note into any in-person communication regarding BGS publication

Personal data privacy notice
In submitting data to INTERMAGNET, your personal data (name, email address, postal address, phone number and institute affiliation) will be held in the INTERMAGNET Observatory Metadata Database as the contact for the observatory. Your personal details are to be used by the British Geological Survey to contact you with regard to collecting observatory data, managing that data and for general communication concerning the observatory. Your email address is the minimum that we require to contact you. Your email address, as well as name, institute, postal address and phone number - if these are provided – would be made publicly available on the INTERMAGNET website as the contact for the observatory.
Your personal data will be handled in line with UK data protection legislation and managed securely. If you would like to know more, including how to exercise your Rights, please see our privacy notice https://www.bgs.ac.uk/legal-and-policy/privacy/.
